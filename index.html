Commandments

  • Thou shall not cheat, and agree to being punished upon doing so
  • Thou shall not collude with other players that result in injustice to the rest of the league
  • Thou shall be respectful, and at times tastefully disrespectful
  • Thou shall do their own research
  • Thou shall keep up with league rules and responsibilities
  • Thou shall play the full season once committed

Decrees of the High Council (League ByLaws)

I. The Houses & Re-Draft Covenant

The realm shall consist of 12 noble houses in re-draft combat (harking back to 10 houses in the year of 2023). If a House decides to forfeit their seat in the realm, they must notify the council swiftly so a worthy successor can fill their lands.

Co-management of a house is fully accepted—this realm is a safe haven for collective rule.

II. The Scoring Grimoire

Battles shall be judged on a Point-Per-Reception (PPR) scoring system:

  • Passing Touchdown: 4 points
  • Receiving or Rushing Touchdown: 6 points
III. Composition of War Rosters

Every active lineup must deploy: 1 QB, 2 RB, 3 WR, 1 TE, 1 FLEX, 1 K, and 1 Def.

  • Maximum limits: 6 WRs max, 5 RBs max
  • Bench capacity: 6 spots total
  • Infirmary (IR): 2 slots. Suspended warriors are strictly barred from the IR. Any player deemed ineligible for IR must be cast out immediately (app restrictions apply).
IV. Treasury & Waivers (FAAB)

Acquisitions from the free agent pool are governed by a Free Agency Acquisition Budget (FAAB):

  • Max war chest: $130 | Minimum transaction bid: $4 | Maximum total waiver adds: 30
  • Waivers process each Wednesday following the week of action.
  • No warriors may be claimed once their battle has commenced or mid-game until waivers clear. No pickups allowed prior to the dawn of Week 1.
V. The Postseason & Iron Throne

6 houses shall advance to the postseason tournament. The top 2 houses secure first-round byes. The highest seed will always go against the lowest seeded matchup until the final. All playoff battles span 1 week.

VI. Trade Diplomacy

The trade deadline falls upon the Saturday following Week 10. All approved trades pass automatically, but trades on game days are forbidden (auto-vetoed until the next peaceful non-gameday).

VII. Amendments & Royal Decree

Any alterations to realm law require a majority vote of 7 or more lords of the realm.

VIII. Tribute & Payouts

Yearly tribute stands at $150 per House (established August 2020), subject to levies for live draft feasts and relics. Payouts distributed as follows:

  • 1st Place (The Crown): $850
  • 2nd Place (Hand of the King): $250
  • Weekly High Scorer Bounties (14 weeks): $50 each
